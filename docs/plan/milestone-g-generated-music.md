## Milestone G: generated music (design 5 Oct; options and plan in `docs/ai-music.md`)

Music built for the film instead of the film fitted to a track: mostly instrumental, so the film's own sound stays clear; any length; intensity that follows the action; the uploaded track's own sung phrases let through only in a few quiet moments (no lyrics of our own). The result is an ordinary music track plus a score (`score.json`), so V5, the rough mix and A3 use it unchanged. The grid is the uploaded track's, so V5 cuts on it without waiting for any of this.
**Steps:** G0 stems (Demucs), re-sequencing the uploaded track to any length and per-bar layering, no generative model (already most of the idea, and the fallback); G1 the intensity curve; G2 the score plan, quiet windows, the original's sung phrases placed by the **director** with a new `sing` draft item (phrase id plus the picture under it; the item is the quiet window), and a **fidelity slider** from "the record" (only the original's audio) to "in its style" (all generated) [default 0.75, "extended"], with per-section pins; G3 ACE-Step 1.5 on the Windows PC for new sections in the original's style; G4 Stable Audio 3 Medium and a bake-off on Legends; G5 fit, check and repair, and the Music panel.
**Decided 5 Oct:** personal film; everything local (a few Gemini Pro calls are fine); no lyrics of our own; tempo and key are the original's; everything runs on the Windows PC (RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB) like the rest of the project; the director chooses the sung moments. **Open:** only the fidelity default (G-D8, [0.75]). **Ready to start (5 Oct):** G0a first (the precise beat tracker shared with V5, key estimation, bar similarity, `edit/remix.py`); see `docs/ai-music.md` 5a.
**Built (5 Oct): G0a.** `music.py` `beat_grid` (dynamic-programming beat tracker that allows drift, refined to the onset peak; every beat and downbeat in seconds), `estimate_key`/`chroma`, `bar_features`/`bar_similarity`; `edit/remix.py` `plan` (bar list to any length: bar 0 first, the track's own last bars last, cheapest joins between similar bars, optional intensity levels) and `render` (equal-power crossfades on the downbeat). Tests: `tests/unit/test_remix.py` (synthetic clicks, drift, chords). **Not yet:** `music.json` does not store the beat list or key; no CLI or endpoint calls it; not yet tried on the Legends track. **Built (5 Oct): G0b.** `edit/stems.py` (Demucs `htdemucs_ft` on ffmpeg-decoded samples, stems kept as FLAC under `music/stems/` keyed by the track's signature; the separator is injectable) and `edit/layers.py` (`layer_gains` per bar in phrases, `open_vocals` windows, `mix` of the re-sequenced stems with ramped gains, `stray_vocal_db` leak check). Tests: `tests/unit/test_stems_layers.py` (fakes and synthetic stems). **Not yet run with real Demucs** (needs the PC: install `demucs` in the music environment, pin it, note time and GPU memory in `progress.md`). **Built (5 Oct): the build and G1.** `edit/music_build.py` (`grid` cached in `music/grid.json` with beats, downbeats, key and per-bar energy; `build` = plan + stems + gains + mix, writing `music/built.flac` and `music/built.json`) and `strata360 music-build FOLDER --length-s N [--vocals START-END]`; `edit/intensity.py` (G1 core, pure: weighted signals, speech ducking, story marks, phrase averaging, four levels with hysteresis; `tests/unit/test_intensity.py`). The beat tracker now carries the grid back to the track's start and on to its end. **Music studio screen (6 Oct):** a new sidebar entry in the app (`web/src/components/MusicStudio.tsx`, endpoints `/api/music/studio` (state), `/analyse`, `/preview`, `/build`, `/api/music/built/audio` in `server/app.py`, plan logic in `edit/music_studio.py`). It shows the track's key, tempo and bars by loudness; a preview of a build for any length with no audio made (which of the track's bars play where, the intensity per phrase from a preset or set by clicking, the layers per bar, the bar-similarity matrix with the chosen stretches outlined, the join costs); moments for the original's singing; a Build button (the `music-build` job, with its log and errors); and the player and score of the built track. `music-build` gained `--preset` and `--levels`; `grid.json` is version 2 and holds the bar similarity. **Footage signals (6 Oct, G1 done apart from scenes):** `edit/intensity_signals.py` gathers, in film time from the plan's segments, the wearer's motion energy (`motion.json`), cheering/shouting/laughter/crowd in the clip's sound (`audio_events.json`, weight 2), pace, climb and heart rate from the race track, and each shot's own energy; the film's start and finish are marked full; segments with clip speech or a voice-over line duck the music (x0.6). The studio's new default preset is "follow the footage" (film length by default), with the signals shown bar by bar under the intensity so the curve can be understood, and `music-build --preset footage`. **Not yet:** the `scenes` stage's energy and mood (MLX only, so absent on the PC), the director's own marked moments, the real voice-over timeline (only the plan's `speech` and `vo` segments), and the curve is not drawn under the music waveform on the Overview; `music.json` does not point at the built track (G2); needs a real run on the PC (Demucs installed) before anything is judged by ear. **Built (7 Oct): G5 and G3's backend.** `edit/music_fit.py`: timing by **lining up rhythms**, not by comparing beat lists (on Legends the tracker locks onto off-beat hits in dense bars and picks differently in the build and the original, up to half a beat): `envelope` (onsets plus the low band doubled), `lag`/`bar_lags` (each bar's window, a quarter beat early so it holds its own downbeat and not the next, cross-correlated within half a beat; a bar matching under 0.3 is not judged), `grid_check` (each built bar against its bar of the original; new bars are left to their take's judgement), `tempo_fit` (a take lined up bar by bar with the audio it replaces, or an ideal pulse; fewer than half its bars lining up means make again; the lags' drift is the tempo error, stretched away with ffmpeg `atempo` up to 3% except for a repaint, whose bars around are fixed; a bar still more than 40 ms off means repaint; a repaint is judged on its bars inside the whole context take), `gain_match`, `splice` (the crossfade starts on the downbeat and runs a beat into the section, so the downbeat itself stays on the grid), `fit_length` (cut to the film with a fade when the cut removes audible music, or padded with silence), `singing_db`, `stale`, `repair` (all pending sections in one batch per round, next seed for the ones that failed; a chosen take is not replaced). `audio/music_gen/`: `caption` (the style plus the level's words, always "instrumental, no vocals"), `spec` (the take key covers the section, mode, caption, the context's source bars and levels, backend, model and steps), `params` (repaint in context with the original's instrumental as reference at fidelity 0.75 and above; a cover of the section's own bars at the reference strength below; text alone at 0), `Generator` (takes cached under `music_gen/<key>/take<seed>.flac` with the fit verdict beside each), `acestep` (runs `acestep_runner.py` with ACE-Step's interpreter: `STRATA_ACESTEP_ROOT`, default `~/Code/ace-step-1.5`, `STRATA_ACESTEP_PYTHON`, `STRATA_ACESTEP_MODEL`, default the turbo model). `music_build.build` now plans the score (`fidelity`, `section_pins`), makes the pieces to generate (each `generate` section split around the opening, the ending and the sung moments, at least 2 bars), fits, matches, splices, cuts to the film and records `sections`, `generated` (mode, seed, takes, gain, vocals), `remade`, `gen_runs` (device, load time, each take's time and peak memory) and `check` (grid, length, stray singing, singing in a generated take, pieces not made). Without a style or ACE-Step, the pieces stay the track's own bars and say why. CLI: `music-build --fidelity F --pin BAR=original|generate --style TEXT --take KEY=SEED --no-generate` (the style is remembered from the last build). Studio: the fidelity slider with its five stops, the style box, the score strip (click a section to pin it to the track, to new bars, or neither; sections not made yet are hatched; sung moments drawn under it), the checks of the built track, and per piece its takes (pick one that kept time, or ask for another; used at the next build). Tests: `tests/unit/test_music_fit.py`, `test_music_gen.py`, more in `test_music_build.py`, `test_music_studio.py`, `test_built_mode.py`, `web/tests/integration/MusicStudio.test.tsx`. **Not yet:** G4 (Stable Audio 3 and the bake-off); bridges as their own kind of section; form b sung moments (a vocal over a generated accompaniment); drawing the score on the Overview's waveform.
